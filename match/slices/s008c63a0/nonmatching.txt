008c6720  554  ScaleBits: complete.  Rescales an sbit glyph from its source ppem to the substitute ppem: computes newWidth = (ppemX*width + subX/2)/subX and newHeight = (ppemY*height + subY/2)/subY with signed idiv, triples the width/bearings under flags 0x3000 or the height/bearings under 0xc000, then runs two in-place passes -- the vertical pass allocates gi->rowBytes*newHeight and calls CopyRows, the horizontal pass allocates the new row bytes and calls PackRows with step = oldWidth -- rescaling the bearings/advance by ppem/subPpem and finishing with bitmapSize = height*rowBytes.
