# SteamStub (EA anti-tamper) entry stub: not compilable C++.
018d2315 91 SteamStub DRM packer code, not game source. The 91-byte extent is hand-written assembly: push all registers, `and esp,-16`, `call 0x18d2390` (the ~8 KB SteamStub section-decryption routine that checks `[ecx+4] == 0xc0dec0df`), restore esp/registers, ret. It also contains an embedded `call $+5 / pop ebx / sub ebx,offset` return-address decoder at 0x18d2347. No C++ source compiles to this (the `call $+5` idiom and the manual stack alignment are assembler constructs), and inline assembly / _emit are rejected by the check. The neighbouring 0x18d2380 (`mov eax, fs:[0x18]; ret`) does match as `__readfsdword(0x18)` (match/slices/s018d2380).
